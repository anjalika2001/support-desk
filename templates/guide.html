User guide

A quick guide for new team members on handling a support request from start to finish.

1. Log the request

  1. On the Tickets page, enter the client, their contact email and phone number (New Zealand numbers, typed after the fixed +64), how they contacted you (phone, email or in person), the category and priority.
  2. Describe the issue clearly: what the client sees, any error message, when it started and who is affected.

2. Choose the right priority

  • High - business stopped or a deadline at risk (e.g. trades not settling, nobody can log in).
  • Medium - work is affected but there is a workaround.
  • Low - minor issue or a question.

3. Check the knowledge base

Search Knowledge base for the same problem. A past fix may solve it straight away.

4. Diagnose and record what you check

Open the ticket and add a note for each step you try. Anyone picking up the ticket can see what has been done.

5. Escalate when needed

If it affects many clients, is urgent, or is beyond first-line support, use Escalate to engineering. Include steps to reproduce, what you've ruled out and the impact.

6. Resolve and confirm

Confirm the fix with the client, then resolve the ticket with a clear note of what fixed it. It becomes part of the knowledge base.

After installing or upgrading

Open System check to confirm the database and tables are set up correctly.
